## Curation Scope

This paper characterises **FgKnr4** in *Fusarium graminearum* and its orthologue **ZtKnr4** in *Zymoseptoria tritici*, both wheat fungal pathogens. The gene is identified via dual WGCNA co-expression network analysis as important for early infection establishment, cell wall integrity, oxidative stress tolerance, and full virulence on wheat.

**Priority genes for curation**:
1. FgKnr4 (*F. graminearum* PH-1) — primary focus
2. ZtKnr4 (*Z. tritici* IPO323) — orthologous validation
