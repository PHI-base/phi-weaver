# Fusarium Effectors

Project notes and curation work related to *Fusarium* effector proteins and their pathogen-host interactions.

## Overview

Project focused on curating *Fusarium graminearum* effector proteins and their pathogen-host interaction mechanisms. *F. graminearum* is the primary causal agent of Fusarium head blight (FHB) in wheat and other cereals, causing significant crop losses and mycotoxin contamination.

### Effector Landscape
- Predicted secretome: ~574-900 secreted proteins
- Multiple effector families target different host cellular processes
- Effectors suppress host immunity during biotrophic phase and promote cell death during necrotrophic phase

## Known Effectors

### FgTPP1 (Trypsin Precursor Protein 1)
- **Function**: Endopeptidase that suppresses host immune responses
- **Localization**: Chloroplasts
- **Mechanism**: Attenuates chitin-mediated MAPK signaling, ROS production, and cell death
- **Expression**: Highly upregulated during wheat spikelet infection
- **Virulence**: Deletion reduces disease progression and colonization
- **Conservation**: Conserved across Ascomycota phylum

### FgSCP (Serine Carboxypeptidase)
- **Function**: Suppresses cell death and modulates plant immunity
- **Roles**: Pathogenicity in wheat/maize, DON biosynthesis regulation
- **Expression**: Upregulated 3 days post-infection during host invasion
- **Phenotype**: Deletion reduces pathogenicity and DON production

### Fg62 Cell Death Effector
- **Function**: Induces plant cell death
- **Expression**: Highly upregulated during early infection stages
- **Host Response**: Triggers cell death in *Nicotiana benthamiana*

### OSP24 (Orphan Protein)
- **Function**: Immune suppression via protein degradation
- **Mechanism**: Targets TaSnRK1α kinase for proteasomal degradation
- **Expression**: Specifically expressed during pathogenesis
- **Effect**: Suppresses BAX- and INF1-induced cell death

### Fg12 Ribonuclease
- **Function**: Secreted ribonuclease that promotes pathogenicity
- **Effect**: Induces cell death and enhances virulence

### Protease Family (FgSLPs)
- **Members**: Five subtilisin-like proteases
- **Function**: Trigger cell death and contribute to virulence
- **Target**: *Nicotiana benthamiana* cell death assays

### Additional Characterized Effectors
- **ARB93B**: Suppresses cell surface-triggered immune responses
- **FGSG_01831**: Suppresses PAMP-triggered ROS production

## Effector Mechanisms

### Host Defense Suppression
1. **Chitin masking**: Camouflage non-protein elicitors to evade recognition
2. **MAPK pathway interference**: Disruption of mitogen-activated protein kinase signaling
3. **ROS suppression**: Inhibition of reactive oxygen species production
4. **Protein degradation**: Targeting host defense proteins for ubiquitin-proteasome degradation

### Virulence Promotion
1. **Cell death induction**: Necrotrophic phase promotion
2. **Mycotoxin regulation**: Control of DON biosynthesis
3. **Tissue colonization**: Enhanced spread through plant tissues

## Current Research Directions

### Recent Advances (2024-2026)
- Functional characterization of FgTPP1 chloroplast targeting
- Discovery of OSP24-mediated kinase degradation mechanism
- Transcriptomic analysis revealing early infection effector expression
- Comparative genomics of effector content across F. graminearum strains

### Knowledge Gaps
- Limited functional characterization of many predicted effectors
- Host target identification for most effectors
- Effector-triggered immunity responses in wheat
- Evolutionary relationships within effector families
